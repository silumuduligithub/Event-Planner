# International marketplace observations

Accessed 2026-10-26. Publication dates unknown. Official public pages only. High confidence in observed text; capability and quality claims remain publisher assertions.

| Publisher and source | Public-page observation | Recommendation (inference) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Peerspace](https://www.peerspace.com/) | Activity, location and date search, including Anytime. Birthday, baby shower, corporate, wedding, workshops and live music categories. | Occasion-first discovery with date flexibility; show rate units. |
| [Tagvenue](https://www.tagvenue.com/) | Event type, guest count and location search; separate venue categories. | Ask guest count when it affects suitability; use progressive filters. |
| [The Bash](https://www.thebash.com/) | Vendor/entertainer and city search; inspiration separate from bands, DJs, photographers, bartenders and caterers. | A direct service path should not require event planning first. |
| [GigSalad](https://www.gigsalad.com/) | Musical acts, entertainers, speakers and event services; quick quote entry. Marketing example puts performer, service, date/time, event description and total together. | Compact request review, retaining separate artist identity. |
| [Zola](https://www.zola.com/wedding-vendors) | Vendor type and location, optional quiz, category/style browsing and planning tools. | Guided planning should be optional; use cross-occasion language. |

Portfolio interactions, selectable planner menus, quantity calculations and authenticated itemized checkout were not inspected. Evently's customizable services are a product requirement, not a claim of competitor parity. No conversion or superiority claims supported. Questions covered; stopped after round one.
