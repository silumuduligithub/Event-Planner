# Indian marketplace observations

Accessed 2026-10-26. Publication dates unknown. Official public page text only; no native apps, authenticated flows, or completed bookings inspected. Confidence is high for the presence of UI text, not for advertised service quality or prices.

| Publisher and source | Public-page observation | Recommendation (inference) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [WedMeGood](https://www.wedmegood.com/) | Vendor type and city search; categories cover venues, photography, makeup, planning/decor and mehndi. Photos, real weddings, blog and invitations coexist in navigation. | Preserve direct service search; place inspiration below the primary task. |
| [WeddingWire India](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-vendors?cmp=B2C) | Category/name and location search. Cards combine name, location, rating/review count, from price and request-pricing action. | Use consistent comparison fields; keep price units explicit. |
| [Meragi](https://www.meragi.com/) | City/destination wedding choice; venue capacity and starting price. Quick-match section uses wedding type, guests and estimated budget with caveats. | Make estimate assumptions visible before inquiry. |
| [BookEventz](https://www.bookeventz.com/), [birthday listings](https://www.bookeventz.com/banquets/mumbai/birthday-party) | Multiple occasion types plus vendor categories; locality, occasion, date, guests and budget search; capacity and per-person pricing. | Support both discovery paths and progressively reveal filters. |
| [VenueLook](https://www.venuelook.com/), [birthday listings](https://www.venuelook.com/birthday-party-venues-in-delhi) | Broad occasion navigation; category chips, capacity, reviews, per-plate and rental pricing. | Keep distinct pricing bases visible and reduce listing prose. |

Marketing statements about shortlisting, managed services and actual estimates were not verified in native applications. No inspected evidence establishes an end-to-end multi-vendor cart. Sources cover the research questions; stopped after round one. Further lead: observe native authenticated comparison behavior with test accounts if that becomes necessary.
