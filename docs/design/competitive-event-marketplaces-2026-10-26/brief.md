# Research brief

Decision: generate a premium, interactive customer mobile UI for booking one service or selected services from an event planner across any occasion.

User-authorized scope: research relevant platforms, then generate the UI. Cover discovery, vendor comparison, service selection and honest request review. Two independent research dimensions: Indian and international official public marketplaces. Source acquisition via web tools; lead visually inspects representative public pages with Playwright. No external communications, signups or bookings.

Bounded pass: two research assistants, five publishers each, one initial round; lead follows visual evidence. This is a focused UX comparison, not a claim to inspect every platform, native app or checkout. No conversion, market-share or pricing-superiority claims. Native implementation remains distinct from an interactive design preview.

The local project has no installed BMAD orchestration scripts; research artifacts are maintained directly. No claim that the entire installed Deep Recon workflow ran.
